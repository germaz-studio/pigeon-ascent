# Pigeon Ascent (Android Port)

[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)

Take care of your pigeon, train their stats, buy equipment, and ascend to defeat increasingly powerful foes in battle!

This repository contains the Android mobile port of **Pigeon Ascent**, ported and maintained by **germaz studio**.

---

## Features
- **Turn-based Pigeon RPG**: Raise your bird, feed it, level up strength, defense, and speed.
- **Dozens of Pigeon Classes**: Unlock and evolve into unique pigeon types with distinct battle skills.
- **Adapted for Android**:
  - Immersive full-screen landscape layout.
  - Responsive touch controls.
  - Fast, battery-friendly native Godot 3.5 runtime.
  - Offline-first with no trackers or intrusive permissions.
